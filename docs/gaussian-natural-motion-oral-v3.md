# Oral v3 and expressive baseline motion — 2026-09-28

User screenshot showed a straight, bright rectangular dental row. Previous v2 used repeated 3x5 crowns with aligned tops, excessive anterior placement and weak shading. V3 tapers the crowns, varies lengths, recesses the arch and lateral teeth, softens edge opacity, and reduces lower-tooth visibility. It preserves the lip-aperture clipping introduced in v2. Both current Emma/Seoyeon entries are upgraded in place; library names remain unchanged. asset.pre-oral-v3.zip and record.pre-oral-v3.json allow rollback.

NaturalMotion previous speaking activity attenuated already small amplitudes. Increased smooth head excursions and gaze variation; introduced energy-driven nod rhythm and separate left/right outer-brow, squint and cheek accents. Affect envelope now ranges .64–1 rather than .88–1, preserving emotion polarity and all articulation channels. Maximum additive speaking speed 14 degrees/second; final head bounds unchanged. This is a procedural facial/head controller, not a learned Ditto motion generator or full-body animation.
